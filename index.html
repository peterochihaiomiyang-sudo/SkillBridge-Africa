Definition of the Project

SkillBridge Africa is a digital platform designed to help skilled refugees showcase their qualifications, validate their professional skills and connect with education, mentorship and career opportunities across Africa.

Why This Project Is Important

Many refugees have valuable education, professional qualifications and work experience. However, they face difficulties getting their qualifications recognized and accessing employment opportunities.

SkillBridge Africa aims to promote equal opportunities, skills recognition, professional development and economic inclusion.

Challenges Facing Skilled Refugees

Credential Recognition

Foreign qualifications may not be recognized. Some refugees may also have missing certificates or limited access to verification services.

Legal and Financial Barriers

Work authorization, administrative requirements and access to financial services can make employment difficult.

Limited Professional Networks

Refugees may struggle to gain local work experience, mentorship and professional connections.

The Problem

Skilled refugees often remain unemployed or work below their qualifications because their education, professional skills and experience are not properly recognized. This results in wasted talent and missed opportunities for communities and employers.

Our Solution

SkillBridge Africa provides a digital pathway where refugees can showcase their qualifications, demonstrate practical skills, track verification progress and discover relevant opportunities.

  • Skills and qualification profiles.
  • Practical skills assessment pathways.
  • Credential verification progress tracking.
  • Access to training and mentorship opportunities.
  • Connections to potential employers and partners.
