Why Track 1 Matters

Ending credential brain waste without lowering quality standards

Skilled refugees often carry years of technical, healthcare, or engineering expertise that goes unrecognised because certificates are unavailable or unfamiliar to local reviewers.

Pillar 01 · Qualification Clarity

Foreign Credential Mapping

Captures issuing institution, field of study, and syllabus details alongside secondary proof like supervisor letters or work badges.

Pillar 02 · Competency Proof

Hands-On Skills Validation

Routes candidates without full paperwork to supervised bench tests, code/portfolio reviews, and structured assessor interviews.

Pillar 03 · Bridging Support

Targeted Re-Certification

Identifies exact local code or regulatory gaps and connects candidates to short bridging modules and exam fee-waiver partners.
