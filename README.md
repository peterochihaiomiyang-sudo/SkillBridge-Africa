# SkillBridge Africa
### Unlocking Talent. Creating Opportunities.

**SkillBridge Africa** is a digital platform designed to help skilled refugees showcase their education, professional experience and practical abilities while exploring pathways to skills validation, credential recognition, mentorship and career opportunities across Africa.

## 1. The Problem

Many refugees have valuable qualifications, technical skills and professional experience. However, they face barriers such as:

- Foreign qualifications that are difficult to recognise.
- Missing or unavailable academic documents.
- Limited access to skills assessments and bridging programmes.
- Challenges understanding work and professional requirements.
- Limited local work experience, mentorship and professional networks.

These barriers can lead to underemployment and wasted talent.

## 2. Our Solution

SkillBridge Africa provides a digital starting point where skilled refugees can present their abilities, explore qualification recognition pathways, learn about skills validation and discover opportunities for professional growth.

Our goal is to help turn displaced talent into recognised skills and meaningful opportunities.

## 3. Main Features

- **Credential Recognition:** Information about pathways for reviewing foreign qualifications.
- **Skills Validation:** A way for users to showcase practical abilities and professional experience.
- **Skills Profiles:** A profile concept for presenting education, experience and skills.
- **Assessment Pathways:** Information about possible bridging programmes and certification routes.
- **Legal and Financial Guidance:** General information and referrals to relevant support organisations.
- **Mentorship:** A pathway for connecting skilled people with professional guidance.
- **Career Opportunities:** A section for exploring education, internships and employment opportunities.
- **Community Partnerships:** Opportunities for institutions, employers and organisations to collaborate.

## 4. How It Works

1. A user visits SkillBridge Africa.
2. The user explores the platform and its available information.
3. The user prepares a profile describing education, skills and experience.
4. The user explores possible assessment and credential recognition pathways.
5. The user discovers mentorship, learning and career opportunities.
6. Relevant institutions and partners can support further verification and professional development.

## 5. Target Users

- Skilled refugees and displaced professionals.
- Refugees with academic qualifications or technical skills.
- Employers seeking skilled talent.
- Educational and professional institutions.
- Mentors and career development organisations.
- Community organisations supporting refugees.

## 9. Expected Impact

SkillBridge Africa aims to:

- Make skills and professional experience more visible.
- Improve awareness of qualification recognition pathways.
- Help displaced professionals identify skills development opportunities.
- Encourage mentorship and professional partnerships.
- Support fairer access to education and employment.

## 10. Future Improvements

Future development may include:

- Secure user accounts and profile management.
- Secure document submission and storage.
- Integration with recognised credential assessment organisations.
- Verified skills assessment and certification partnerships.
- Employer and mentor dashboards.
- Opportunity matching.
- Impact measurement and reporting.

## 11. Current Project Status

This is a hackathon prototype built to demonstrate the SkillBridge Africa concept.

The current website uses a front-end demonstration. Registration information is not yet securely stored or transmitted, and the platform does not independently verify qualifications or issue official certificates.

These capabilities will require secure backend development and partnerships with authorised institutions.

## 12. Project Vision

We believe talent has no borders. Skilled refugees deserve fair opportunities to demonstrate their abilities, develop professionally and contribute to their communities.
